🌎 Think Like a Scientist

Scientists use data to identify patterns. A pattern does not automatically prove why something happened. Scientists need reasoning and evidence to explain the pattern.

✍️ Construct a CER

Question:

How does temperature affect the amount of water that evaporates?

C

Claim

Make a clear statement that answers the question.

Sentence starter:

As temperature increases, the amount of water lost...

E

Evidence

Use at least two pieces of evidence.

R

Reasoning

Explain why your evidence supports your claim.

Think about this connection:

temperature → heat energy → evaporation → water vapor

Sentence starter:

This evidence supports my claim because...

Build Your CER

Claim: Answer the question.

Evidence: Give numbers or information that support your claim.

Reasoning: Explain the science connecting your evidence to your claim.
