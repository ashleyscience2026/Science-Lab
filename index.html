🔬 Investigation: Evaporation Lab

Investigation Question

How does temperature affect the amount of water lost through evaporation?

Lab Procedure

  1. Choose a temperature.
  2. Choose the trial number.
  3. Click Run Trial.
  4. Observe the virtual beaker.
  5. Read the ending-water measurement.
  6. Record the measurement in your Scientific Notebook.
  7. Enter the same measurement into the data table.
Important:

Do not enter a number before running the trial. Scientists collect observations before analyzing them.

✍️ Construct a CER

Scientific Question

How does temperature affect the amount of water that evaporates?

Use your Scientific Notebook and data table. Your CER should use evidence from your investigation.

🟥 CLAIM

Answer the scientific question in one clear sentence.

Sentence starter:

As temperature increases, the amount of water lost through evaporation...

⬛ EVIDENCE

Use at least two pieces of evidence from your investigation. Include numbers from your data.

🟨 REASONING

Explain why your evidence supports your claim. Connect your data to the science of evaporation.

Think about the chain:

Temperature → particle energy → evaporation → water vapor

CER Help

Claim: What happened?

Evidence: What numbers or information prove it?

Reasoning: Why does that evidence make sense scientifically?
