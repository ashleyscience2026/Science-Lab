📐 Measurement Rules

1. Always include a unit.

A number by itself is incomplete.

25 could mean 25 cm, 25 mL, 25 g, or 25°C.

2. Read the scale carefully.

Look at the numbered markings first. Then count the smaller divisions between them.

3. Estimate one more digit when appropriate.

Scientists often record one estimated digit beyond the smallest marked division.
