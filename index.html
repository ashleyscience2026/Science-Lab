Essential Question

How do scientists use observations, measurements, mathematics, and evidence to explain changes in the natural world?

Scientists don't just guess.

Scientists observe, measure, record data, look for patterns, and use evidence to explain what happened.

Our Mystery

A container begins with 100 mL of water. Later, only 77 mL remains.

Where did the missing water go?

📖 Read Like a Scientist

Your teacher will give you a printed scientific reading about evaporation and the water cycle.

The reading will be printed for you. Use the questions below while you read.

Vocabulary

Evaporation: liquid water changing into water vapor.

Water vapor: water in its gas state.

Condensation: water vapor changing into liquid water.

Energy: the ability to cause change or do work.

Evidence: information used to support a scientific claim.
