⚖️ What Is Density?

Density describes how much mass is contained in a certain amount of volume.

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

D = m ÷ V

Units:

Mass may be measured in grams (g).

Volume may be measured in milliliters (mL) or cubic centimeters (cm³).

A common density unit is g/mL or g/cm³.

🧠 Think About It

Two objects can have the same volume but different masses. The object with more mass has the greater density.

Density compares mass to volume.

🧮 Practice 1: Calculate Density

A rock has a mass of 60 g and a volume of 20 mL.

60 g ÷ 20 mL = 3 g/mL

What is the density?

⭐ Challenge

🔍 Density Triangle

D = M ÷ V

M = D × V

V = M ÷ D

🧮 Practice 2: Find the Mass

A material has a density of 2 g/mL and a volume of 15 mL.

What is its mass?

Mass = Density × Volume

Mass = 2 × 15 = 30 g

🧪 Practice 3: Find the Volume

A sample has a mass of 80 g and a density of 4 g/mL.

What is its volume?
