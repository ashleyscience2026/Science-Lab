👀 Accuracy Matters

When reading a measuring tool, scientists should:

  • Use the correct tool.
  • Use the correct unit.
  • Read the scale carefully.
  • Record the measurement immediately.
  • Include the appropriate number of decimal places.

⚖️ Apply Your Measurement Skills

Scientists sometimes use measurements to calculate other properties of matter.

Density

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

Density describes how much mass is packed into a certain volume of matter.

Density = — g/mL

Divide the mass by the volume.

For example, if an object has a mass of 20 g and a volume of 10 mL:

20 ÷ 10 = 2 g/mL

⭐ Challenge Question

Two objects have the same volume. Object A has a mass of 50 g. Object B has a mass of 80 g.

Which object has the greater density? Explain your reasoning.
