🎯 Accuracy vs. Precision

Accuracy

How close a measurement is to the accepted or true value.

Precision

How close repeated measurements are to one another.

Imagine the target below.

🎯

A group of measurements can be precise without being accurate.

🧠 Check Your Understanding

These measurements are:

A. Accurate and precise

🧮 Measurement Math

Scientists use mathematics to compare measurements, find differences, and calculate averages.

Example:

A graduated cylinder contains 47 mL of water. After water is added, it contains 68 mL.

68 mL − 47 mL = 21 mL

So, 21 mL of water was added.

Problem 1

A sample has a mass of 84.6 g. Another sample has a mass of 71.2 g.

What is the difference in mass?

Problem 2

A student records these temperatures:

21°C, 23°C, 22°C
